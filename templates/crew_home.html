WHY THIS BUFFER?

Expected demand: confirmed choices plus undecided passengers distributed using the past-flight meal mix. Standard safety stock: 5% of total forecast demand, rounded up once for the flight. {% if festivals %}{{ festivals[0].name }} reserve: 10% of forecast demand for festival-aligned meals only, rounded up once and split across those rows.{% endif %} There is no minimum add-on per category, so a zero-demand option does not receive a random extra meal.

ⓘ The {{ not_chosen }} passenger{% if not_chosen != 1 %}s{% endif %} without a saved meal are forecast by the historical mix as a group; no individual passenger is assigned a meal. The history shown here is sample data for the demo.
